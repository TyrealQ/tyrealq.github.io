## Research Focus

My research addresses problems at the intersection of service marketing and digital communication with a focus on the esports and sports industries. I also leverage applied artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to analyze both structured and unstructured data across physical and digital platforms, offering perspectives on consumer behavior in today’s interconnected environments.
