## Join Our Team

The Applied Artificial Intelligence in Sport Lab at Indiana University Bloomington is growing. Our team includes [John Ruichen Yang](https://mukj1.github.io/) and Willie Weizhe Li, and I am recruiting PhD students to join us.

PhD students in the lab receive a funded assistantship that includes a competitive annual stipend, a full tuition waiver, and health insurance. Through the assistantship, you will have the opportunity to teach both in-person and online courses, starting as a teaching assistant. For research, you will have data and computing resources from my research grant and the Indiana University [high-performance computing (HPC) systems](https://uits.iu.edu/services/technology-for-research/high-performance-computing-and-storage/index.html). Each project starts from a research question, and you will learn to choose the data, structured or unstructured, and the methods that best answer it. You will lead projects based on your own research interests and have the opportunity to contribute to mine, and you are expected to publish at least one first-authored article in a top sport management journal or a leading business journal (rated A or above by ABDC), such as Willie Weizhe Li's [article in the *Journal of Sport Management*](https://doi.org/10.1123/jsm.2025-0076). With department and lab support for travel, you will present at both domestic and international conferences.

If you are interested, please email me at [tyqian@iu.edu](mailto:tyqian@iu.edu) with your CV and your answers to these questions:

1. What led you to reach out to me, and what do you hope to gain from joining the lab?
2. How would my mentorship support your development, and what skills, experiences, or ideas would you bring to the lab?
3. Which of my projects or publications stood out to you, and how would you extend or improve it?
4. How do you define meaningful research? Give one example of a research question you find meaningful, and explain why.
5. What makes sport a distinct context for research, rather than one more marketplace for general management, marketing, or consumer behavior research?

These questions have no definitive answers, and my own views on them continue to evolve. Please be yourself and write in your own words. Using AI to polish your writing is fine, but do not let it replace your reasoning. Heavily polished responses tend to read as mechanical, and what I hope to see is your voice and your ideas.
