Open Resources

{% capture open_resources %} I manage knowledge spaces in the following areas: - Sport Management Research: Examination of [consumer psychology and behavior](https://doi.org/10.1080/14413523.2021.1930700) across sport, esports, and [related ecosystems](https://doi.org/10.1123/jsm.2025-0076), including but not limited to participation, [spectating](https://doi.org/10.1080/16184742.2019.1630464), and [broader consumption practices](https://doi.org/10.1177/21674795241262667), as well as the effects of [gamification](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2022.02.082) and the [dynamics of user-generated content (UGC)](https://github.com/TyrealQ/Break50), alongside the development of [accessible pedagogical frameworks](https://github.com/TyrealQ/NLP4ALL) that translate research insights into [inclusive learning experiences](https://github.com/TyrealQ/AI-Conversation) and advance the democratization of knowledge across diverse communities. - Natural Language Processing (NLP): Application of both traditional and advanced NLP techniques, including [lexicon based and rule based methods](/publication/Smithsonian), [word embeddings](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretconser.2025.104520), [aspect based sentiment analysis (ABSA)](https://github.com/TyrealQ/Experience-is-all-you-need), [topic modeling](https://github.com/TyrealQ/Esports-2023-Asian-Games), and the integration of [large language models (LLMs)](https://github.com/TyrealQ/Break50) for semantic interpretation, classification, and inference. - Computer Vision (CV): Analysis of images and video that combines conventional feature based methods with [large multimodal models (LMMs) for image understanding](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/b641ec3b-4650-49e4-8afb-20e2789ad21f), cross modal reasoning, and [video analytics](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tyrealqian/TGL_content_classification), carried out with [q-multimodal](https://github.com/TyrealQ/q-skills/tree/main/skills/q-scholar/q-multimodal), a toolkit that extracts visual, audio, and semantic features from media. - Datasets: Curation and analysis of multimodal datasets encompassing [UGC](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tyrealqian/Tripadvisor_stadium_reviews_P5_schools), news media, corporate reports, and other [textual](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tyrealqian/Zeigler), visual, and audiovisual sources. - Agent Skills: Development of specialized agentic modules ([q-skills](https://github.com/TyrealQ/q-skills)) that autonomously execute end-to-end tasks, including analytical pipeline execution ([q-eda](https://github.com/TyrealQ/q-skills/tree/main/skills/q-scholar/q-eda), [q-tf](https://github.com/TyrealQ/q-skills/tree/main/skills/q-scholar/q-tf)), social media content generation ([q-infographics](https://github.com/TyrealQ/q-skills/tree/main/skills/q-infographics)), and the production of formatted research reports ([q-scholar](https://github.com/TyrealQ/q-skills/tree/main/skills/q-scholar)). Resources available on: [GitHub](https://github.com/TyrealQ), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/tyrealqian), [OSF](https://osf.io/dx8bc/), and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@tyrealq?si=nAMjGEzRCEXQ7SBt). {% endcapture %} {{ open_resources | markdownify }}
