excerpt: >
  Which post wins on Instagram, the athlete in uniform or the person behind it? With my collaborators, I collected nearly every Instagram post from 690 NBA and WNBA players, 107,693 posts in all, and sorted each one as the jersey (the competitor), the hoodie (the person), or both. About half of the posts in both leagues mix the two, and the mix keeps growing. In the NBA, fans reward a clear ladder: game posts earn the most likes, mixed posts come second, and personal posts come last. In the WNBA there is no ladder until the players are split by stardom: for players who are not yet All-Stars, mixed posts earn 23% more likes than their personal posts, and for All-Stars the gap disappears.
