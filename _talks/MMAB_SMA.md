  I am glad to share our research on athlete self-presentation at SMA 2026 in Baltimore. Our talk, "[A Multi-Lens Computational Account of NBA Athlete Branding on Instagram](https://canva.link/hp1bs2lgaqjhaff)," examines mixed Instagram posts, which show the athlete as a professional (frontstage) and as a person (backstage) at the same time. We rated the captions and the visuals of 56,752 posts from 486 NBA athletes separately under the Model of Multimodal Athlete Branding (MMAB) and found that about half of the posts were mixed. Mixed posts received more likes and comments than exclusive backstage posts but fewer than exclusive frontstage posts, and the gap to frontstage posts was largest when the personal content appeared in the images and videos rather than only in the caption.
