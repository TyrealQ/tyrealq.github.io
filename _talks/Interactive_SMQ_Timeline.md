This figure comes from a project that maps the published record of *Sport Marketing Quarterly* from 1999 to 2026: the subjects the journal has covered and the way its articles cite one another. The manuscript is in preparation, so the full analysis will be shared after publication. The figure below shows one piece of it, the journal citing itself.

What you can do with the figure:

- Hover a dot to see the article's title, authors, year, and topic, with up to five of the articles it cites and up to five that cite it.
- Click a dot to highlight every article it cites and every article that cites it. The full lists appear under the figure, and each listed article can be clicked in turn.
- Click a family in the legend to hide the others, or click a period header to keep only the citations made by that period's articles.
- Use the switches, the search box (author surname or title word), and the size key (click a circle to set a citation threshold). Reset clears everything, and Escape clears the selection.
